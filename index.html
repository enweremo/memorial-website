01 / THE OBITUARY

A LIFE OF GRACE & PURPOSE

A beautiful life.
An enduring legacy.

✺

Her story does not end with goodbye. It lives on in the love she gave and in the hearts that will always remember her.

With hearts full of sorrow, yet grateful for the gift of her life, the family announces the passing of Late Mrs. Veronica Uzoamaka Chiaha (née Ugwu), who transitioned to glory on November 4, 2026.

We gather in remembrance not only to mourn her departure, but also to celebrate the moments that made her presence so precious. The familiar warmth of family, the conversations shared, the celebrations enjoyed and the quiet acts of care are memories that time cannot take away.

As we honour her memory, we give thanks for the bonds of love that connected her to those around her. Her passing leaves a space in our lives, but the affection, gratitude and treasured recollections she inspired will remain with us.

Those she leaves behind

She is survived by her beloved children and grandchildren, and is lovingly remembered by her relatives, friends and well-wishers. Together, we cherish her memory and the place she will forever hold in our hearts.
